# Student Life OS — Project Constitution & Sitemap

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy

**Student Life OS** is the unified academic command center and habit defense workspace for university students and disciplined knowledge workers. Built upon the **Not To Do** routine protection engine, it integrates:
- **Academic Spine**: Immovable university lecture schedules, laboratory sessions, midterms, and exam deadlines.
- **Defensive Productivity**: Inverted habit tracking protecting against vulnerable procrastination traps ("Not To Do").
- **1-Tap Dynamic Schedule Repair ("Fix My Day")**: Algorithmic rescheduling of flexible routines into calculated free idle gaps without disturbing locked academic events.
- **Multimodal Timetable Vision**: Automatic syllabus and timetable ingestion powered by Gemini Vision.
- **High-Density Focus Instruments**: Pomodoro focus timer, Wheel of Life holistic balance radar, Task Manager priority board, Weekly TimeTable matrix, and Academic Calendar with 2-way device synchronization.
