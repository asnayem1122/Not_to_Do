A high-density focus soundscape and ambient acoustic player workstation integrated into the Student Life OS and Pomodoro station.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
1. Canvas must be `#0E1015` dark background with `#161922` container cards and `#232734` borders.
2. Maintain high-density tactical minimalism with JetBrains Mono for audio waveforms, decibel meters, and timestamps, and Plus Jakarta Sans for titles.
3. Status pills: Emerald green (`#10B981`) for active audio channels, Crimson red (`#F43F5E`) for binaural beats (Gamma 40Hz focus), Sky blue (`#38BDF8`) for rain/nature frequencies, Amber gold (`#F59E0B`) for lo-fi cafe chatter.
4. Clean rounded corners (8px to 12px) with 1px border outlines.

**Page Structure:**
1. Header: Breadcrumbs back to Student Life OS, Live Sound Engine status badge, Master Volume slider, Session Preset selector ("Deep Flow", "Reading Quiet", "Coding Sprints").
2. Active Soundscape Mixer: Multi-channel sliders for Rain on Window, Forest Stream, Cyberpunk Cafe, White Noise, and 40Hz Gamma Binaural Frequencies.
3. Audio Visualizer & Waveform: Monospace frequency spectrum visualizer with tactical Hz levels and live decibel telemetry.
4. Focus Preset Vault: Curated focus mixes with one-tap activation and sleep/session timer controls (25m Pomodoro sync).
5. Pomodoro Integration Rail: Mini sync timer linked to current study sprint.
